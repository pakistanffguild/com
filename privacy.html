Privacy Policy

PAKISTAN FF GUILD (“we”, “us”) is an independent Free Fire community and is not affiliated with Garena. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you submit the guild application form we collect your game name, Free Fire UID, WhatsApp number, email (optional), preferred role, rank and message. We use this only to contact you and manage guild recruitment.

AI assistant

If the AI assistant is enabled, the messages you type in the chat are sent to an AI service to generate a reply. Please do not share passwords or sensitive personal information in the chat.

Analytics & cookies

We use Google Analytics and essential storage to understand visits and keep the app working offline. You can disable cookies in your browser or device settings.

Sharing

We never sell your data. Application details are processed through our form provider (Web3Forms) and delivered to guild leaders. We do not share your data with other third parties except where required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing pakistanffguild@gmail.com or messaging the guild leader on WhatsApp.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences following Free Fire’s own age rules; we do not knowingly collect data from young children.
